# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Lumen Store to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and welcoming
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before reacting

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing other people's private information
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or trolling
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@deepcodecreate](https://github.com/deepcodecreate) through GitHub. All reports are handled privately and fairly.
